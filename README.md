## 📖 What is HomeCar?

HomeCar is a free, open-source application that turns your Android Auto display into a personalized dashboard for your smart home. It uses a full-screen web view to show your Home Assistant interface directly on your car's screen, so you can control lights, thermostats, cameras, and more—all while keeping your eyes on the road.

Imagine this: You're driving home, and with a single tap on your car's touchscreen, you adjust the garage door, turn on the porch lights, and set the thermostat to your preferred temperature. HomeCar makes that possible.

Whether you're a smart home enthusiast or just getting started, HomeCar is designed to be simple, safe, and fully customizable. No programming skills required.

## 🧪 Troubleshooting

**HomeCar doesn't show up on Android Auto**
- Ensure your phone is running Android 8.0 or later.
- Check that Android Auto is enabled on your phone.
- Verify that HomeCar is installed on your phone (not just your PC).

**The dashboard is blank or not loading**
- Double-check the URL in HomeCar settings. It should include the full address, including the port (e.g., `http://192.168.1.100:8123`).
- Make sure your Home Assistant instance is online and accessible from your phone's network.

**The interface is too small or too large**
- Use the zoom controls in your Home Assistant interface if available.
- Adjust the screen scaling in HomeCar's settings menu.

**HomeCar crashes or freezes**
- Restart the application.
- Update to the latest version from the [releases page](https://github.com/Actiniaalternanthera1370/HomeCar/releases).

If problems persist, please check the GitHub repository's Issues section or open a new issue for support.
